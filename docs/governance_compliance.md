## 1. The Sovereign Key Model
[cite_start]To mitigate counterparty risk, the University retains ultimate ownership. [cite: 10305]
-   **Master Key:** Held by the University (Owner). [cite_start]Can revoke Operator access at any time. [cite: 10308, 10309]
-   **Operator Key:** Held by Offbeat Options (Manager). [cite_start]Used for daily management and yield monitoring. [cite: 10308]

## 2. Reserve & Solvency
-   [cite_start]**1:1 Reserve Mandate:** All outstanding tokenized rewards must be backed 1-to-1 by identifiable reserves. [cite: 9028]
-   [cite_start]**Segregated Funds:** Client capital (The Anchor) is held in segregated accounts, never commingled with Company operating funds. [cite: 9029]

2.  [cite_start]**Temporal Truth:** The student wallet has not exceeded the **Velocity Limit** (e.g., >2 claims in 24 hours). [cite: 10312]
